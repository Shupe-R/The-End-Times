This was a device designed by an alien race to collect data on black holes and the lifecycles of a star. This specific one was part of a project researching larger stars. Since the device was sent to a foreign star to the race, they needed a way to keep it active without needing to go and physically refuel it. They landed on collecting expelled energy from the star it would orbit. The energy it would take in would fuel it for much longer than it was being fuled Eventually though, funding was cut and the department was shut down. But, the device still remained active but unmaintained. After a few hundred years (<--maybe change this) the device experienced a malfunction and the system that was taking input of energy from the star suddenly sent an output right into it almost imediately causing it to go [[supernova]] and turn into a [[black hole]].
